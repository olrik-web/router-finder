About

Route Finder makes loop routes that start and end at the same point. Pick a start, choose how far you want to go, and download the route for your watch or phone. Every press of the button gives a new route.

Routes come from openrouteservice, search from Nominatim, and the map and its data from OpenStreetMap contributors.
